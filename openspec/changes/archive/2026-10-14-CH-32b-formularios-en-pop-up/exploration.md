## Shape that fits (no decision needed)

- **One pop-up mechanism, five users.** Extract CH-32a's open / ask-then-close / discard logic into one console helper parameterised by the backdrop node, the first field, a dirty predicate and a close callback; Consultas keeps its behaviour and ids. Each form gets its own `div#…-dialogo.zd-dialog-backdrop` with its own ids (guarded in `IDS`), a title naming the action, the main action and «Cancelar» at the foot, and the shared discard strip («Hay cambios sin guardar.» + «Descartar cambios» + «Seguir editando»).
- **Dirty means "something typed".** Tenant: name non-empty. User: email or name non-empty. Connection: any field differs from the blank form (port 5432 and the first "Vía" count as blank). Wizard: a connection or template chosen, or anything typed in step 2.
- **Openers move to the page header** (`.zd-pagehead__actions`), one primary action per screen: «Nuevo tenant», «Nueva conexión» (already there), «Nuevo usuario», «Nueva automatización». The wizard keeps its two steps and stepper inside a wide pop-up (same console rule as Consultas).
- **Closing on success.** Tenant and user: the pop-up closes, the page notice says what was created; the new user's password keeps showing once in `#usuario-secreto` on the page, as today. Wizard: closes and shows the existing confirmation. Errors (400, 409) keep the pop-up open with the typed values and the message inside it (CH-32a's `avisarEnConsulta` pattern). Connection: see D3.
- **Tenant switch** closes any open pop-up without asking, as CH-32a does.
- **«Reponer clave» confirms inline**, like «Desactivar» in the same row: «Reponer clave de {correo}» + «Cancelar», with a line saying the user's sessions will be closed. Inline destructive confirmations are outside DEC-173.
- **Connection name in the automations table.** Load `GET /conexiones` together with the list; show the name; if the id is not found (deleted connection), show the short id. The wizard's existing load can reuse the same cache.
- **Schedule in words.** Port the three DEC-129 presets' reverse (daily, Mon–Fri, Mon–Sat at HH:MM) into the console script, mirroring `frecuenciaDeCron`; a preset shows the words plus the cron in `.zd-cron` mono (D2), a custom expression that matches no preset shows the cron alone.
- **Specs.** Modify the five `query-console` requirements above, widen "Create and Edit Forms Open in a Pop-up (DEC-173)" to the five forms, and add scenarios for choosing the new tenant, confirming «Reponer clave» and the automations row. `docs/02-mapa-de-changes.md` gains the CH-32b row.
- **Out of scope.** Editing automations (audit obs. 8, F3), typed confirmation of a tenant's baja (F3), the design guide's C-05 text (proposal to the guide, not code). The audit's "pasada visual a 360/1280 px" is not added (standing user preference).

## Decisions (resolved 2026-10-14)

- **D1 — Choosing the tenant just created** (audit obs. 11 suggests «Crear y elegir»).
  - (a) Two actions at the pop-up's foot: «Crear tenant» and «Crear y elegir». Explicit, keeps today's behaviour as default.
  - (b) Always choose it after creating. One action, but creating a tenant silently changes context and wipes the open screens.
  - (c) Create only; the success notice offers «Elegir {nombre} como activo». No extra button in the pop-up, one more click.
- **D2 — Horario column.** The user asked for words *instead of* the cron; the design guide (C-09) asks for words *plus* the cron in mono.
  - (a) Words only for presets; the cron only for custom expressions.
  - (b) Words plus the cron in mono for presets (guide C-09); the cron alone for custom expressions.
- **D3 — «Nueva conexión» after «Guardar y probar».** Today the panel stays open showing the test result.
  - (a) Close the pop-up and show "Guardada. Prueba: …" in the page notice, also when the test fails (the connection is saved either way).
  - (b) Keep the pop-up open showing the result, with «Cerrar» replacing «Cancelar». Deviates from "closes on success".

## Size
